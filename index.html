01 / ABOUT

Research driven by
real-world impact.

Hello! I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Cheikh Anta Diop University (UCAD), Senegal. My research explores how Artificial Intelligence and mathematical modeling can support the epidemiological understanding, prediction and surveillance of schistosomiasis.

Working within a One Health perspective, I am particularly interested in combining environmental, socio-epidemiological and biomedical data to develop predictive models and identify areas at risk. I also explore computer vision methods for the analysis of microscopic images.

Beyond research, I am committed to scientific collaboration, capacity building and the development of AI research in Africa.
