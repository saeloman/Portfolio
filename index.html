About Me

Hello! I am Mohamed-Saiaf Aliamini, a PhD student in Computer Science at Cheikh Anta Diop University (UCAD), Senegal. My research explores the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Neglected Tropical Diseases (NTDs), with a particular focus on schistosomiasis.


My doctoral research investigates AI-based approaches for predicting schistosomiasis prevalence by integrating environmental, socio-epidemiological and microscopy data within a One Health framework. I am also interested in computer vision for microscopic image analysis and in ecological modeling to identify areas suitable for intermediate-host snails.


Beyond research, I am passionate about scientific collaboration, capacity building and the development of AI solutions addressing public health challenges in Africa.
